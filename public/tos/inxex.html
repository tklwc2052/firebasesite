Terms of Service

Last updated: October 5, 2026

By using Avern Chat, you agree to follow these Terms of Service and all server rules.

General Terms

  1. All users must follow all rules and these Terms of Service.
  2. Moderators may change or add rules as the server changes.
  3. Repeated violations may result in more severe punishment.
  4. Failure to follow these rules may result in the listed punishment or another punishment considered appropriate by the moderation team.
  5. Bans and mutes are different. Bans may be permanent unless appealed. Ban evasion may result in additional or IP bans. Mute evasion, including editing messages while muted or creating another account, may result in a ban.
  6. Do not share the server link without permission from kL_, Doge, or Crims.
  7. Harassing others for personal information is prohibited and may result in a two-hour mute or ban.
  8. Sharing your own personal information is discouraged. Anything shared online may remain available permanently.
  9. Questions should be directed to a moderator.
  10. The server owner may be able to view messages, including DMs and deleted messages. Do not send anything you would not want reviewed by a responsible adult.
  11. These rules apply across the chat and server at all times.

Rules

  1. No harassment or threats. Violations may result in any-length or IP bans.
  2. Report threats to kL_, Doge, or Crims as soon as possible. Save screenshots and send them privately to a moderator.
  3. Do not abuse powers from a special rank. Penalty: 60 to 90 minute ban or mute and possible removal of powers.
  4. No ban evasion. Penalty: 60 to 80 minute ban and possible IP ban.
  5. Do not shame anyone for something they said or did. Penalty: 60 to 90 minute ban or mute.
  6. Do not use commands without a reason. Commands such as /poll are allowed. Penalty: 50 to 60 minute mute.
  7. Do not spam commands except for approved testing. Penalty: 45 to 60 minute mute or ban.
  8. No false accusations of rulebreaking. Penalty: 45 to 55 minute mute or ban.
  9. Do not beg for higher ranks or access. Penalty: 30 to 40 minute mute.
  10. Do not make fun of other users. Penalty: 30 to 40 minute mute.
  11. No inappropriate or disrespectful jokes, pictures, or symbols. Penalty: 30 to 40 minute mute.
  12. Do not start pointless arguments that consume server resources. Penalty: 15 to 25 minute mute or up to a 30 minute ban.
  13. No spamming. Penalty: 5 to 30 minute mute.
  14. Use #klisabum only for serious suggestions about the Minecraft server, chat, or related services. Penalty: 15 to 30 minute mute.
  15. Admins may not clear channels without permission or a valid reason. Inform kL_ first. Penalty: 5 to 10 minute mute.
  16. Extreme profanity, abusive language, or harassment is prohibited. Penalty: 30 minute to one-hour mute or ban.
  17. Do not beg or harass moderators to remove a punishment. Penalty: 15 to 30 minute mute. Repeated violations may result in a ban.
  18. Deleting messages connected to an offense may result in a 15 minute mute. Deleting a message does not guarantee that it is gone.

Personal Information

Do not share another person's personal information, including names, ages, or similar identifying details. Think carefully before sharing your own information because strangers may see it and it may remain online. If personal information must be shared, use DMs. These rules apply at all times.

Romantic Partners

Users with partners in the chat must remain civil and keep private or concerning conversations out of public channels. Harassing moderators about a punishment involving your partner is still harassment. Public flirting may result in mutes or, for repeated violations, bans. Use common sense.

Admins and Moderators

Admins may carry out punishments across the server. Moderators can make mistakes, but users may not harass them. Extreme abuse of moderator powers may result in removal from the moderation team. Do not spam other moderators while requesting a moderator's removal.
