HOW TO PLAY

Movement: A / D to move. Space to jump (jump against walls to wall-jump!).

Mining & Interactions: Select a slot on your hotbar, then press SHIFT + W/A/S/D to interact with the block in that direction.

  • Slot 1 (Pickaxe): Mine blocks to collect ores & resources.
  • Slot 2 (Sword): Hold Shift to attack nearby monsters.
  • Slot 3 (Hand/Table): Place your Enchanting Table, or open it once placed.
  • Slot 4 (Dirt): Place Dirt back into the world.
  • Slot 5 (Torches): Place Torches to light up the dark.

Hotkeys:

  • 1-0: Quickly select Hotbar Slots.
  • E: Open the Village Trader (Shop).
  • T: Open Chat to talk to other players.
  • Tab: View the active Player List.
  • B / N: Drop Bombs (20G) or Dynamite (10G).

Dig deeper to find rarer ores like Diamond and Lapis. Watch out for monsters in the dark!
